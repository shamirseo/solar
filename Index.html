FAQ

Questions people ask before going solar

Still unsure?

Talk to a real person. We'll answer honestly, even if solar isn't right for you.

Call 1300 499 708
How much does a solar system cost?
It depends on system size, components and your roof. After a quick chat about your usage we give you a clear, itemised quote with no hidden extras.
Are there government rebates available?
Federal and some state incentives can reduce the upfront cost of solar and batteries. We'll explain which apply to you and factor them into your quote.
How long does installation take?
Most home installs are completed in a day once approvals are in place. We'll give you a realistic timeline when you accept your quote.
Do I need a battery?
Not always. A battery makes the most sense if you use a lot of power in the evening or want backup. We'll tell you honestly whether it's worth it for you.
What happens if something goes wrong later?
Call us. Our local support team helps with questions, faults and servicing long after your system is switched on.
Which areas do you service?
We install across Victoria, New South Wales and Western Australia. Not sure if you're covered? Give us a call.
